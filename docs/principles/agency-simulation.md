# Agency, simulation and causal claims

The core constitution's requirements language, definitions and applicable rules govern this module. Its Modules table determines any additional applicable modules. One adopted revision binds the core and its modules.

### SIM-1 — The first slice completes the claimed causal loop

**Trigger:** An autonomous, agentic, adaptive, or simulation claim depends on decisions producing consequences and feedback.

**Rule:** For an autonomous, agentic, adaptive, or simulation claim whose value depends on decisions producing consequences and feedback, the first acceptance-bearing slice MUST complete the smallest causal loop that bears the claim: a candidate decision is valid under current authority; execution produces an inspectable consequence, explicit failure, or deliberate non-action; relevant perception or feedback occurs; any claimed memory, belief, plan, or adaptation persists at the fidelity relied upon; and a later decision or human effect materially depends on that result. Planning, retrieval, movement, state mutation, event delivery, or narration demonstrated separately are seam probes unless one is itself the selected primary outcome. The loop MUST cross the causal scale and semantic boundary named by the selected effect. An individual action loop does not bear a claim about household, organizational, settlement, regional, ecological, economic, or long-horizon consequences unless the slice reaches that boundary and the later decision or human effect depends on it. The slice need not prove every population, final scale, or final architecture; it MUST establish the exact claimed loop and effect before broader scale.

When memory, retrieval, ranking or adaptation bears the claim, also apply the search-adaptation module's triggered rules.

### SIM-2 — Causal and robustness claims need intervention and variation

**Trigger:** A claim concerns a causal tendency, emergent pattern, counterfactual effect, or behavior expected across seeds, configurations, populations or nearby model variants, whether or not labelled simulation.

**Rule:** When the accepted claim concerns a causal tendency, emergent pattern, counterfactual wake, or behavior expected across seeds, configurations, populations, or nearby model variants, exact replay of one trajectory is insufficient. Before material scale or product-like hardening, compare the relevant intervention with a control, prior state, or other credible counterfactual and exercise bounded independently selected seeds, cases, or nonessential perturbations proportionate to the claim. The evidence MUST show what direction or family of outcomes is attributable to the intervention, what diversity is legitimate, which changes are intended to matter, and whether the claimed effect survives declared nonessential variation within accepted qualitative bounds. No universal replication count, statistical threshold, or demand for trajectory similarity applies. A deterministic one-off whose complete accepted claim is the exact trace is exempt. A product that deliberately seeks chaotic divergence, fragile equilibria, or butterfly effects MAY accept sensitivity as part of the human effect, but it MUST distinguish causally legible sensitivity from arbitrary dependence on incidental identifiers, ordering, fixtures, or unexamined implementation details.

Artificial evaluators bearing the claim also trigger `EVAL-2`. Improvement attributed to adaptation or search also triggers `SEARCH-3`.

### SIM-3 — Resolution follows the accepted effect

**Trigger:** A simulation spans more than one causal scale.

**Rule:** When a simulation spans more than one causal scale, no resolution level has presumptive authority. The representation used for each current phenomenon MUST be justified by the selected effect and the distinctions its accepted consumers actually use. Individual people, households, organizations, settlements, cohorts, aggregates, scheduled processes, and summary events are all legitimate authority levels. Lower-level completeness MUST NOT gain authority merely because it appears more fundamental, realistic, reusable, or capable of eventually generating higher-level behavior. Individual detail is required when the accepted case depends on identity, embodiment, custody, knowledge, timing, relationship, consent, or interaction that aggregation would erase; aggregate or summarized behavior cannot substitute for a promise about those distinctions. Conversely, an individual-level substrate MUST NOT be treated as a prerequisite for a household-, market-, settlement-, ecological-, or regional effect that can be tested credibly at a coarser level. Any promotion, demotion, aggregation, or disaggregation relied upon by a consumer MUST preserve the authoritative continuity and causal ancestry that consumer needs, without transferring knowledge, rights, or detail that the source representation did not establish.

### SIM-4 — Runtime LLM authority has an explicit state and action boundary

**Trigger:** An LLM participates in runtime narration, state changes, proposals or action selection.

**Rule:** When an LLM participates, the accepted boundary MUST distinguish facts it may narrate freely, state changes it may authoritatively produce under the accepted runtime policy, changes it may only propose, and consequence classes governed by deterministic or human authority. The LLM MUST NOT cross that boundary merely by narrating a convenient success, failure, discovery, injury, movement, or other governed outcome. Conversely, deterministic state MUST NOT expand to control narrative detail that no current accepted consequence or consumer requires.

Where a controlled system defines or mechanically derives legal actions, valid targets, preconditions, timing windows, resource limits, or consequence classes, those constraints are authoritative. An LLM MAY select, rank, sequence, or parameterize within the accepted boundary, and MAY propose a novel action for separate validation, but it MUST NOT make an unavailable action or target executable by naming, scoring, or narrating it. Any semantic matching or ranking at this boundary remains subject to `SEM-1`. Free-form LLM action authority remains a legitimate product or bounded experiment only when explicitly accepted and when its risk-bearing slice proves the required validity, failure, consequence, recovery, and intervention behavior as applicable.

Ranking fitness also triggers `SEARCH-1`; ordinary coding-agent implementation alone does not trigger this runtime rule.
